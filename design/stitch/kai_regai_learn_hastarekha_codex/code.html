Canonical Rekhas

Three-layer traditional textual documentation

5 Lines Standard

Jeeva Rekha (Life Line)

Tradition: Vedic & Classical
Observed Morphology:

Deep, unbroken arch encircling the thenar eminence (Mount of Venus) from radial index border to wrist rascette.

Traditional Meaning:

Constitutional endurance, somatic vitality, and ancestral life force (prana).

Source Citation:

Samudrika Shastra • Adhyaya 4, Sloka 12

Mastri Rekha (Head Line)

Tradition: Vedic & Western
Observed Morphology:

Transverse crease originating with or near Jeeva Rekha, traversing the palm toward upper Mount of Chandra.

Traditional Meaning:

Discriminative intellect (buddhi), mental stamina, and analytical pragmatism.

Source Citation:

Brihat Samhita • Hastalakshana 68.18

Hridaya Rekha (Heart Line)

Tradition: Vedic & Classical
Observed Morphology:

Superior transverse crease originating under Mount of Mercury and terminating towards Jupiter or Saturn mount.

Traditional Meaning:

Capacity for devotional constancy, emotional magnanimity, and interpersonal integrity.

Source Citation:

Bhavishya Purana • Brahma Parva 26.9

Karma Rekha (Fate Line)

Tradition: Vedic & Classical
Observed Morphology:

Vertical conduit rising from palm base ascending towards the Mount of Saturn (Shani Sthana).

Traditional Meaning:

Vocation, deliberate ethical determination, and manifestation of self-directed karmic fruit.

Source Citation:

Samudrika Tilaka • Chapter 2, Sloka 31

Surya Rekha (Sun Line)

Tradition: Classical Indian
Observed Morphology:

Vertical secondary line running parallel to Fate line terminating directly beneath the ring finger mount.

Traditional Meaning:

Aesthetic distinction, societal recognition, and illumination in chosen pursuits.

Source Citation:

Garga Samhita • Hastarekhadhikara
