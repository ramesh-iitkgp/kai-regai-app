## Brand & Style

The design system bridges timeless Vedic chiromancy (*Hastarekha Shastra*) with cutting-edge neural computing. It evokes reverent wonder, scientific confidence, and deep introspection. The experience avoids carnival clairvoyance tropes in favor of an opulent, celestial research-lab instrument: measured, ancient, and technologically transcendent.

The design movement combines **Dark-Mode Celestial Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Glassmorphism**:
- Obsidian, deep void backdrops paired with multi-depth translucent slate surfaces.
- Precise 1px hairline borders carrying faint metallic and lunar reflections.
- Ethereal, tightly restrained directional auroras and luminescent biometric vector overlays.
- High-contrast editorial pacing that balances cosmic breathing space with hyper-dense diagnostic telemetry.

## Colors

The palette leverages high-order contrast ratios against deep space grounds, pairing energetic psychic spectral emissions with the warm patina of ancient temple brass.

### Base Canvases & Surfaces
- **Canvas Deep Midnight (`#080A12`)**: The foundational canvas; absorbs distraction and grounds biometric light phenomena.
- **Surface Dark Slate Blue (`#111522`)**: Primary card background, providing foundational structure.
- **Elevated Surface (`#171C2C`)**: Active cards, bottom sheets, navigation bars, and flyout dialogues.
- **Border Trim (`rgba(214, 181, 106, 0.12)`)**: Hairline gold-tinged borders structuring floating dark plates.

### Accents & Sacred Geometry
- **Primary Violet (`#8B72FF`) & Deep Violet (`#5D46C7`)**: The primary brand driver, indicating AI scanning cycles, activation nodes, and primary CTA vectors.
- **Antique Gold (`#D6B56A`) & Warm Gold (`#E8CA7A`)**: Secondary prestige metal; flags ancient interpretations, Vedic scripture references, and high-value epiphanies.

### Semantic Palm Lines & Data Overlays
- **Life Line (`#A855F7`)**: Vitality and constitutional strength.
- **Head Line (`#00D2FF`)**: Cognitive clarity, logic, and analytical intellect.
- **Heart Line (`#F43F5E`)**: Emotional depth, resonance, and relational paths.
- **Fate Line (`#FBBF24`)**: Karmic trajectory, focus, and life vocation.
- **Sun Line (`#F59E0B`)**: Radiant energy, public renown, and creative vitality.

### Text & Feedback
- **High-Emphasis Text (`#F6F1E7`)**: Sacred Ivory, eliminating pure white glare.
- **Muted Text (`#B9B4AA`)**: Subdued bone ivory for diagnostic copy and metadata.
- **System States**: Success (`#6FCF97`), Warning (`#E5B96B`), Destructive/Error (`#E97979`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy intentionally bifurcates into two distinct voices:

1. **The Vedic Mystic (Playfair Display)**: Used strictly for thematic chapter headers, prophecy titles, oracle statements, and reflective quotes. It must maintain comfortable, generous line-heights to ensure high legibility in light-on-dark environments.
2. **The Modern Biometric Analyst (Manrope)**: Serves all body copy, numerical readings, telemetry outputs, interactive labels, and guidance cues. Manrope offers modern geometric clarity with open counters, rendering cleanly on high-density OLED mobile displays.

### Typographic Rules
- Use `quote-wisdom` with italic styling for ancient Sanskrit translations or direct biometric revelations.
- All `label-sm` elements should utilize uppercase treatment to demarcate diagnostic metric categories (e.g., `HEART LINE LENGTH`, `MOUNT OF APOLLO`).
- Never set long-form paragraphs in Playfair Display; reserve it entirely for headline-level storytelling.

## Layout & Spacing

A mobile-first architectural grid built for one-handed thumb interaction within dynamic viewport constraints.

### Mobile Screen Parameters
- **Grid Layout**: 4-column fluid mobile grid with `1.25rem` (20px) outer canvas margins and a `1rem` (16px) gutter.
- **Thumb Reach Architecture**: High-frequency interactive controls reside strictly within the lower 42% of the viewport. Story readings and diagnostic visualizations claim the top 58%.
- **Safe Dynamic Spacing**: Bottom-sheet drawers and fixed action panels must incorporate safe-area dynamic inset padding (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom) + 0.75rem`).

### Desktop / Tablet Presentation
When viewed on larger canvases (tablet or web preview), content is constrained to a single centered vertical viewport column of `440px` maximum width, preserving the intimate ergonomics of handheld biometric hardware.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical darkness tiers rather than structural dropshadows. Surfaces get brighter as they float closer to the user, imitating an illuminated optical deck.

### Tonal Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Deep Ground - `#080A12`)**: The limitless canvas behind camera viewports and spatial charts.
- **Level 1 (Docked Containers - `#111522`)**: Structural cards and segmented lists. Faint perimeter border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Floating Modals & Cards - `#171C2C`)**: Active analytical reports, popups, and elevated selector cards. Framed with `1px solid rgba(214, 181, 106, 0.18)` and an ultra-diffuse ambient aura: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`.
- **Level 3 (Interactive Controls / Tooltips - `#20273D`)**: Draggable nodes, floating toolbars, and context balloons.

### The Sacred Scan Glow
When a palm landmark is recognized or an insight card is highlighted, elevation is augmented with a localized, restrained radial glow:
- **Violet Resonance**: `0 0 24px -2px rgba(139, 114, 255, 0.25)`
- **Golden Epiphany**: `0 0 20px -2px rgba(214, 181, 106, 0.22)`
- Never cast hard, sharp shadows. Depth must feel like light cast over obsidian stone.

## Shapes

The interface balances organic human biomechanics with clean technological instrumentation. 

- **Primary Cards & Containers**: Set to `rounded-lg` (16px / 1rem) for an ergonomic, polished silhouette.
- **Input Fields & Button Enclosures**: Set to `rounded` (8px / 0.5rem) or `rounded-lg` (12px / 0.75rem), ensuring finger targets feel intentional and stable.
- **Interactive Badges & Biometric Indicators**: Full pill capsules (`rounded-full`) for categorical tags, line telemetry status, and capture counters.
- **Reticle & Scanner Corners**: Crisp, chamfered corner lines (L-bracket vectors) for the active camera scanning frame, signaling AI precision.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Mystic CTA**: Solid gradient background from `#8B72FF` to `#5D46C7`. Text in `#F6F1E7` (`label-md`). Height 52px for mobile ergonomics. Inner glow hairline border `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`.
- **Golden Ritual CTA**: Surface tinted in Antique Gold (`#D6B56A`) with dark typography (`#080A12`, font weight 700) reserved for major revelation reveals or premium unlocks.
- **Secondary Ghost**: Surface `#171C2C`, hairline border `1px solid rgba(214, 181, 106, 0.3)`. Text in Ivory `#F6F1E7`. Active state dims to `rgba(214, 181, 106, 0.08)`.

### Biometric Chips & Line Indicators
- Height 32px, pill-shaped.
- Background `rgba(17, 21, 34, 0.8)` with backdrop-filter blur (8px).
- Displays line identifier dot (6px round, colored according to semantic line palette) alongside value in `label-sm` (e.g., "HEAD LINE • 94% ALIGNED").

### Cards & Reading Insights
- **Wisdom Plate**: Layered Level 1 (`#111522`) card with a thin top border gradient (`#D6B56A` fading to transparent). Body text in `body-md` Ivory, title in `headline-md` Playfair Display.
- **Scanning Matrix Card**: Dark slate background overlaid with faint concentric biometric contour lines at 4% opacity.

### Form Inputs & Date/Time Selectors
- Background `#111522`, `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Height 48px, radius 8px.
- Focus state transition: border color switches to `#8B72FF` with subtle outer violet bloom (`0 0 8px rgba(139, 114, 255, 0.3)`).
- Input labels rendered above field in `label-sm` with muted ivory tone.

### Lists & Palm Characteristic Selectors
- Divided with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- Trailing chevrons colored in muted antique gold (`rgba(214, 181, 106, 0.6)`).
- Leading circular glyph frames in `#171C2C` with subtle center icon representing mounts (Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, Apollo).

### Scanning Reticle (Specialized Mobile Component)
- Centered 4:5 aspect viewfinder matching ergonomic hand placement.
- Dynamic responsive SVG lines that auto-snap over palm contours in real time using Semantic Line hex values, driven by real-time tracking points.
