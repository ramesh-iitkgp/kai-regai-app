## 2. In-Depth Phase Audit Findings

### 1. Source Corpus Integrity
* **Selected Historical Indian Corpus**: Anchored in peer-reviewed scholarly translations (Kenneth G. Zysk's critical edition of *Pāṇilekhā* in *Śārdūlakarṇāvadāna*, Varāhamihira’s *Bṛhat Saṃhitā*, *Hastasañjīvana*). Spurious modern web claims have been systematically purged.
* **Selected Historical Western Corpus**: Built upon William G. Benham (1901) and Cheiro (1916). Both works are confirmed to reside in the **public domain worldwide**.
* **Modern Indian Corpus**: Grounded in recognized 20th-century treatises (Dr. N.D. Shrimali, Gopesh Kumar Ojha, V.A.K. Ayer) using clean-room factual extraction without literary infringement.

### 2. Epistemological Decoupling
* **No Raw Image to LLM**: Confirmed. All interpretation paths require intermediate serialization into `PalmFeatureModel`.
* **No Invented Palmistry**: Every sample interpretation rule links directly to a vetted `source_id` with specific chapter and page references.
* **Preservation of Disagreement**: The Conflict System explicitly retains divergent views between classical Sanskrit sources and Western characterology rather than creating an artificial hybrid.

### 3. Hand Scanner vs. Guided Questionnaire Guardrails
* The **Hand Scanner (~10 seconds)** is strictly constrained to `CV_DETECTABLE` features (Hand outline, 21 landmarks, Life Line violet, Head Line blue, Heart Line rose, Fate Line gold, Sun Line amber). It is prohibited from generating claims regarding subtle or tactile features.
* The **Guided Questionnaire (Mode 2)** provides the interactive interface for capturing `USER_INPUT_REQUIRED` attributes (thumb flexibility, pad firmness, subtle sacred markings).
