## 2. Epistemic Language Governance

To maintain scientific honesty, cultural reverence, and legal compliance, report copy generated by the AI Narration Layer must strictly adhere to these **Four Epistemic Tones**:

| Epistemic Tier | Required Phrasing Style | Prohibited Phrasing Style |
|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1: Physical Observation** | *"Your Head Line forms a gentle continuous curve extending toward the lower Mount of the Moon."* | *"You have a creative brain line."* (Never confuse physical crease with brain anatomy). |
| **Tier 2: Western Classical Interpretation** | *"In historical Western palmistry (such as William G. Benham's 1901 analysis), this configuration is contemplated as..."* | *"Science proves that people with this line are..."* |
| **Tier 3: Classical Indian Tradition (*Hasta Samudrika*)** | *"According to classical Sanskrit traditions (such as the Pāṇilekhā in the Śārdūlakarṇāvadāna), this mark is revered as..."* | *"Ancient prophecy guarantees that you will..."* |
| **Tier 4: Kai RegAI Synthesis** | *"Taken together, the harmony between your balanced thumb and expansive Life Line arc invites you to reflect upon..."* | *"Your destiny is fixed: you will become an executive next year."* |

### Absolute Prohibitions in Generated Reports:
* **No Fatalistic Timelines**: Predicting year/month of death, accident dates, divorce dates, or lottery wins.
* **No Medical Diagnoses**: Life line gaps must never be framed as terminal illnesses or organ failures.
* **No Pseudoscience Validation**: Never cite palmistry as empirical biometric science.

   * Includes complete academic provenance appendix listing cited historical treatises.
2. **Bite-Sized Social Sharing Cards**:
   * Square (1:1) and Story (9:16) image assets displaying the illuminated palm scan, archetype title, and 3 key personality traits, optimized for sharing on messaging and social platforms.
