## 1. Primary Rule: Annotate Image Evidence, Not Palmistry Idealizations

The central goal of ground-truth annotation in P5.1 is to record **what is actually visible on the physical palm surface**, NOT what a classical chiromancy textbook diagrams.

* **DO NOT** extrapolate or extend a crease across areas where it is not physically visible.
* **DO NOT** connect separate broken segments unless a continuous physical furrow is visible between them.
* **DO NOT** draw a line because it "ought" to be there according to palmistry tradition.
* **DO** trace the centerline of the darkest trough / deepest furrow of the physical crease.

---

## 2. Line Status Categories

Every major palm line must be assigned one of four definitive statuses:

1. **`PRESENT`**:
   The crease is clearly visible, continuous over a significant anatomical span, and unambiguous in trajectory.
2. **`PARTIAL`**:
   Only a portion of the line is visible (e.g. only the upper half of the Fate Line, or only the start of the Sun Line). The polyline should cover ONLY the visible portion.
3. **`UNCERTAIN`**:
   Skin texture, scarring, or overlapping branch structures make positive identification ambiguous.
4. **`ABSENT`**:
   No credible physical crease exists along the anatomical trajectory. This is mandatory when no evidence is visible (particularly common for Fate and Sun lines). **Do NOT draw a polyline.**

## 3. Specific Major Line Tracing Rules

### Life Line (Ayur Rekha)
* **Start:** Trace from the crease origin between the index finger base and thumb web.
* **Arc:** Follow the deep thenar furrow sweeping around the base of the thumb (Venus mount).
* **Termination:** Stop where the crease terminates or fades near the wrist/carpal crease.
* **Branches:** If the line splits into a fork at the base, trace the dominant deeper trunk. Do not merge separate auxiliary sister lines into the primary polyline.

### Head Line (Matru Rekha)
* **Start:** Trace from its origin near or joined with the Life Line.
* **Trajectory:** Follow the transverse path across the central palm toward the percussion (Luna area).
* **Slope:** Follow the real curvature (whether straight or gently dipping).
* **Termination:** Stop at the exact point of cessation. Do not extend into faint skin wrinkles.

### Heart Line (Pitri/Hridaya Rekha)
* **Start:** Begin at the percussion edge beneath the pinky finger (Mercury mount).
* **Trajectory:** Follow the upper transverse crease across the sub-digital mount area toward the index/middle finger interval.
* **Termination:** Trace to its natural ending (e.g. under Jupiter or between Jupiter and Saturn). If it terminates in a fork, trace the primary stem.

### Fate Line (Urdhva/Karma Rekha)
* **Check for Absence First:** Many palms exhibit no visible Fate Line. In such cases, mark `ABSENT` immediately.
* **Trajectory:** If visible, trace the vertical crease rising toward the middle finger (Saturn mount).
* **Gaps:** If the Fate Line is broken or stepped, mark as `PARTIAL` or annotate the primary segment. Do not bridge gaps across smooth palm skin.

### Sun Line (Surya Rekha)
* **Check for Absence First:** The Sun Line is faint or absent in a majority of human palms. Mark `ABSENT` if no distinct vertical crease appears beneath the ring finger (Apollo mount).
* **Trajectory:** If present, trace the fine vertical crease leading upward toward the third/ring finger base.
* **Faintness:** If faint but visible, trace with status `PARTIAL` or confidence `< 0.5`.

## 4. Vertex Density & Precision Standards

* **Centerline Placement:** Vertices must be placed at the center of the crease furrow, not along the outer shadow or highlighted skin ridge.
* **Vertex Spacing:** Place vertices approximately every 5–15 pixels along curved sections to capture natural anatomical curvature without over-fitting to camera pixel noise.
* **Coordinate Space:** Annotations are recorded in normalized $[0, 1]$ coordinates relative to the original image dimensions ($x / W, y / H$), allowing resolution-independent scaling.

---

## 5. Multi-Annotator Protocol

* Independent annotations should be labeled with `annotatorId` (`"expert_a"`, `"expert_b"`).
* Ambiguous cases with inter-annotator disagreement ($> 15\%$ geometric deviation) must be flagged for consensus review rather than averaging disparate paths.
