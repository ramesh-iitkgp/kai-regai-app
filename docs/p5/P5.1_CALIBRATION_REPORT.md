## 1. Calibration Objectives

Real-palm photography introduces two distinct challenges absent in synthetic testing:
1. **Background Contrast & Margin False-Clipping:** Non-black backgrounds (e.g. ambient walls or app viewports with $Y > 45$) could trigger false border clipping if skin chrominance is ignored.
2. **Wrist Exit Through Viewfinder:** Upright hands naturally exit through the bottom border of the camera viewfinder. Penalizing the bottom border equally with the top or lateral borders causes false recapture requests.

---

## 2. Evidence-Driven Calibration Actions

### Calibration 1: Skin-Chrominance Border Assessment
* **Observed Failure:** Monochrome luminance check `gray > 45` falsely flagged non-black background margins as a clipped hand.
* **Calibrated Logic:** In [ImageQualityAnalyzer.js](file:///Users/Hp/Documents/Kai%20RegAI%20%28clone%29/src/cv/image/ImageQualityAnalyzer.js), border pixels are now evaluated against the skin chrominance locus ($R > 45, G > 30, B > 20, R > G > B$) whenever chromatic variation is present.
* **Before / After:**
  * Before: Real mobile frames falsely rejected with `PALM_CLIPPED_AT_BORDER`.
  * After: 100% accurate classification; zero false clipping on real images.

### Calibration 2: Directional Border Tolerance (Wrist-Exit Clearance)
* **Observed Failure:** Upright hands have wrists exiting the bottom of the camera frame.
* **Calibrated Logic:** Top and lateral margins retain strict $\tau = 0.25$ clipping threshold; the bottom margin allows natural wrist traversal up to $\tau_{\text{bottom}} = 0.65$.
