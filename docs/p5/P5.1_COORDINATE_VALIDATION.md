## 1. Mathematical Formulation

To guarantee that detected crease polylines align precisely with real photographs without systematic displacement or scaling drift, four coordinate spaces are formalized:

### 1. Original Image Space $\mathbf{P}_{\text{orig}} = (X_{\text{orig}}, Y_{\text{orig}})$
Arbitrary camera resolution $[0, W_{\text{orig}}] \times [0, H_{\text{orig}}]$.
If clamped by max processing resolution $M = 1024$:
$$s_{\text{clamp}} = \frac{\min(M, \max(W_{\text{orig}}, H_{\text{orig}}))}{\max(W_{\text{orig}}, H_{\text{orig}})}$$
$$\mathbf{P}_{\text{img}} = \mathbf{P}_{\text{orig}} \cdot s_{\text{clamp}}$$

### 2. Rectified Palmar Patch Space $\mathbf{P}_{\text{patch}} = (px, py) \in [0, 512]^2$
Let palm centroid be $(c_x, c_y)$, orientation angle $\theta = \text{atan2}(dx, dy)$ (wrist to middle MCP), and scale $s_{\text{roi}} = \frac{2 \cdot r_{\text{palm}}}{512}$.
For a point $\mathbf{P}_{\text{img}} = (x, y)$:
$$\begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & \sin\theta \\ -\sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} x - c_x \\ y - c_y \end{bmatrix}$$
$$px = 256 + \frac{u}{s_{\text{roi}}}, \quad py = 256 + \frac{v}{s_{\text{roi}}}$$

### 3. Normalized Patch Space $\mathbf{P}_{\text{norm}} = (x_{\text{norm}}, y_{\text{norm}}) \in [0, 1]^2$
$$x_{\text{norm}} = \frac{px}{512}, \quad y_{\text{norm}} = \frac{py}{512}$$

### 4. SVG Overlay Space $\mathbf{P}_{\text{overlay}} = (x_{\text{overlay}}, y_{\text{overlay}}) \in [0, 360] \times [0, 450]$
$$x_{\text{overlay}} = x_{\text{norm}} \cdot 360, \quad y_{\text{overlay}} = y_{\text{norm}} \cdot 450$$

---

## 2. Inverse Transformation Chain (Overlay $\to$ Original)

$$\begin{aligned}
x_{\text{norm}} &= \frac{x_{\text{overlay}}}{360}, \quad y_{\text{norm}} = \frac{y_{\text{overlay}}}{450} \\
px &= x_{\text{norm}} \cdot 512, \quad py = y_{\text{norm}} \cdot 512 \\
u &= (px - 256) \cdot s_{\text{roi}}, \quad v = (py - 256) \cdot s_{\text{roi}} \\
\begin{bmatrix} \Delta x \\ \Delta y \end{bmatrix} &= \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \end{bmatrix} \\
X_{\text{orig}} &= \frac{c_x + \Delta x}{s_{\text{clamp}}}, \quad Y_{\text{orig}} = \frac{c_y + \Delta y}{s_{\text{clamp}}}
\end{aligned}$$
