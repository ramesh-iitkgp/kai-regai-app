## 2. Baseline P5 Performance (Pre-Calibration)

* **Initial Gate Result:** Real camera frames were prematurely rejected with `status: "RECAPTURE_REQUIRED"` due to `PALM_CLIPPED_AT_BORDER`.
* **Root Cause:** `ImageQualityAnalyzer.analyzeFraming` used a simple grayscale threshold ($Y > 45$), treating medium-gray room backgrounds ($Y \approx 50$) as clipped hand skin.
* **Detection Bypass Test:** When the framing check was bypassed, the underlying CV engine successfully extracted Life (0.782 conf), Head (0.844 conf), and Heart (0.792 conf) creases on the identical real frame in under $96\text{ ms}$.

---

## 3. Failure Analysis

* **Dominant Failure Mode Identified:** False-positive border clipping on non-black domestic backgrounds and natural wrist exit off the bottom of the camera frame.
* **Failure Taxonomy Code:** Addressed under the pre-gating optical validation layer (preventing downstream F11/F6 errors).
* **Absence Detection:** Fate and Sun lines were correctly identified as absent (`NOT_DETECTED`) on real frames lacking visible Apollo/Saturn creases, with zero decorative hallucinations.

---

## 4. Calibration & Parameter Changes

* **Parameters Modified:**
  1. `ImageQualityAnalyzer.analyzeFraming`: Upgraded from scalar luminance ($Y > 45$) to skin-chrominance locus detection ($R > 45, G > 30, B > 20, R > G > B, \Delta(R, G) \ge 8$).
  2. Directional Border Tolerance: Maintained strict $\tau = 0.25$ on lateral and distal margins; calibrated bottom margin to $\tau_{\text{bottom}} = 0.65$ to accommodate natural wrist emergence.
* **Before / After Comparison:**
  * Before: 0% real camera frames cleared the optical quality gate (100% false recapture rate).
  * After: 100% real camera frames cleared the optical quality gate; $95.95\text{ ms}$ average processing latency.
  * Regression Impact: 0 regressions across all 30 automated tests in the test suite.

## 8. Remaining Limitations

1. **Extreme Hand Rotation ($> 45^\circ$):** The current skin-chrominance bounding heuristic works reliably within $\pm 25^\circ$. Rotations exceeding $45^\circ$ require MediaPipe landmark coordinates.
2. **Flash Specular Saturation:** Direct close-range flash reflections saturate skin pixels, properly triggering `RECAPTURE_REQUIRED`.
3. **No Interpretation or Fatalism:** The engine strictly outputs geometric polylines and physical observables, obeying all epistemic rules of Kai RegAI.
