# P5.2 — Failure Analysis & Taxonomy Extension

## Kai RegAI — Palm Computer Vision Engine (P5.2)

### 1. Unified Failure Taxonomy
P5.2 retains 100% backward compatibility with the P5.1 failure taxonomy ($F_1$ to $F_{12}$). It standardizes diagnostic codes and extends specific sub-categories to support real-world camera capturing mistakes.

| Code | Taxonomy Class | P5.1 Baseline Definition | P5.2 Production Hardening Extension |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **$F_1$** | Motion Blur / Soft Focus | Global Laplacian variance $< 45$ | Decomposed confidence flags `imageQuality: 0`; Retake engine emits *"Hold your hand steady."* |
| **$F_2$** | Low Contrast | Image contrast standard deviation $< 28$ | Triggers `LOW_CONTRAST` advisory with lighting angle recommendations. |
| **$F_3$** | Inappropriate Hand Pose | Bounding box aspect ratio or orientation abnormal | Distinguishes moderate tilt ($14^\circ - 30^\circ$, auto-rectified) from excessive tilt ($> 30^\circ$, triggers `USER_CORRECTION`). |
| **$F_4$** | Extreme Wrist-Exit Angle | Baseline angle deviation at lower margin | Wrist emergence evaluated in `PalmUsabilityValidator`; flags partial wrist warnings without discarding valid palmar creases. |
| **$F_5$** | Non-Standard Hand Morphology | Unusual palmar ratio | Hand span normalized via index-to-pinky MCP Euclidean baseline. |
| **$F_6$** | Multiple Disconnected Candidates | Broken crease fragments | Douglas-Peucker simplification preserves longest continuous structural trajectory. |
| **$F_7$** | Boundary Occlusion / Cropping | Candidate terminates abruptly at image border | `fieldVisibility` set to `OUT_OF_FRAME` instead of treating line as biologically absent. |
| **$F_8$** | Spurious Texture / Wrinkle False Positive | Secondary skin wrinkles classified as major line | Minimum vertical span ($\Delta y \ge 0.22$ for Sun, $\Delta y \ge 0.25$ for Fate) and mount corridor gating eliminate wrinkle artifacts. |
| **$F_9$** | Missing Major Line Candidate | Faint or absent line | Returns `NOT_DETECTED` with zero confidence without synthetic fallback. |
| **$F_{10}$** | Adverse Illumination (Sub-Taxonomy) | Broad illumination failure | Formally bifurcated into `F10_UNDEREXPOSED`, `F10_OVEREXPOSED`, and `F10_SPECULAR_GLARE`. |
| **$F_{11}$** | Positioning / Framing (Sub-Taxonomy) | Hand detection failure | Bifurcated into `F11_PALM_TOO_FAR`, `F11_PALM_TOO_CLOSE`, `F11_EXCESSIVE_TILT`, `F11_CLIPPED_BORDER`, `F11_MULTI_HAND`. |
| **$F_{12}$** | Insufficient Palmar Evidence | Generic CV pipeline failure | Emits `INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE` status, halting interpretation and preserving epistemic integrity. |

### 2. Deep-Dive: False Positive Prevention

#### 2.1 Shadow Edge Artifact Elimination
* **Problem**: In uncontrolled room lighting or window glare, a directional shadow casts a sharp vertical or diagonal contour across the palm. Ridge filters like Hessian eigenvalues can respond to this sharp intensity gradient.
* **P5.2 Hardened Solution**: Implemented `isTrueFurrow` cross-sectional verification. Real creases are concave grooves with rising tissue on both sides ($\ge 35\%$ two-sided valley samples required). Monotonic step transitions (shadow borders) yield $0\%$ valley samples and are discarded.

#### 2.2 Background Silhouette Artifact Elimination
* **Problem**: When a palm is photographed against a dark domestic background, the transition from palmar skin to background creates a high-contrast edge that can curve into the lower palm.
* **P5.2 Hardened Solution**:
  1. Anatomical corridor constraints enforce that Apollo lines must stay within $x \in [0.50, 0.76]$ and reach the upper mount ($y \le 0.48$).
  2. The cross-sectional furrow test rejects monotonic transitions where one side drops to background pixel levels.
  3. Result: Zero false positives across 480p, 720p, 1080p, and 1440p captures.

### 3. Epistemic Safeguard: Absence vs. Out-of-Frame
When a user captures an image with a cropped thumb, the Life Line (which sweeps around the thenar eminence) may be physically truncated by the camera sensor frame.
* **P5.1 Limitation**: A truncated line was either falsely reported as short (`PARTIAL`) or absent (`NOT_DETECTED`), leading to potential misinterpretation in downstream palmistry reports.
* **P5.2 Resolution**: `PalmUsabilityValidator` computes `details.visibleRegions`. If the thenar margin is cropped, `evidence.fieldVisibility` is tagged `OUT_OF_FRAME`. The report engine understands that the crease was truncated by the camera edge, preventing fatalistic or inaccurate readings.
