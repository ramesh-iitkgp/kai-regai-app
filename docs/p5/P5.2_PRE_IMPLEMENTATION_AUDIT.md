## 1. Executive Summary & Audit Mandate

In strict accordance with the P5.2 Master Engineering Directive, this document records the comprehensive forensic audit of the existing Kai RegAI Computer Vision pipeline. The P5/P5.1 architecture is frozen, validated, and locked with 30/30 automated tests passing and zero regressions.

P5.2 is **not** an architectural rewrite and does not replace the validated pipeline. P5.2 is an **additive hardening, quality-gating, and robustness layer** designed to make the engine resilient against uncontrolled real-world captures (diverse resolutions, aspect ratios, natural skin chrominance, non-black/domestic backgrounds, lighting extremes, scale variations, and capture failures) without hallucinating palm creases.

## 3. Data Contracts & Canonical Interfaces

### 3.1 Types (`src/cv/types.js`)
* `PalmImage`: `{ width, height, data: Uint8ClampedArray, channels: 4, colorSpace: 'srgb', metadata }`
* `ImageQualityResult`: `{ score, sharpness, illumination, contrast, glare, framing, resolution, occlusion, passed, reasons, recommendations }`
* `HandDetectionResult`: `{ detected, handedness, boundingBox, confidence, landmarks }`
* `PalmROI`: `{ mask, roiPatch, roiSize, boundingBox, landmarks, orientation, scale }`
* `MajorPalmLine`: `{ type, status, polyline, confidence, continuity, visibility, evidence }`
  * Statuses: `DETECTED | WEAK | PARTIAL | NOT_DETECTED | AMBIGUOUS`
  * Types: `LIFE | HEAD | HEART | FATE | SUN`
* `PalmVisionResult`: `{ status, imageQuality, hand, palm, lines, processing }`
  * Master statuses: `SUCCESS | RECAPTURE_REQUIRED | NO_HAND | INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE | PROCESSING_ERROR`

### 3.2 Coordinate Spaces (`src/cv/geometry/CoordinateTransform.js`)
The P5.1 pipeline established lossless bidirectional transformations across 5 discrete coordinate spaces:
1. **Original Image Space** $(X_{\text{orig}}, Y_{\text{orig}})$: Raw camera capture coordinates.
2. **Normalized Image Space** $(X_{\text{img}}, Y_{\text{img}})$: Clamped coordinates $(\le 1024\text{px})$.
3. **Rectified Palm ROI Patch Space** $(X_{\text{patch}}, Y_{\text{patch}})$: $512\times 512$ square patch rotated so wrist $\to$ middle MCP is vertical.
4. **Normalized Patch Space** $(x_{\text{norm}}, y_{\text{norm}}) \in [0, 1] \times [0, 1]$: Relative coordinates for anatomical priors.
5. **SVG Overlay Space** $(x_{\text{overlay}}, y_{\text{overlay}}) \in [0, 360] \times [0, 450]$: P4 mobile UI overlay frame.

## 4. Subsystem Forensic Details

### 4.1 Ingestion & Normalization (`ImageIngestion.js`)
* Clamps inputs where $\max(W, H) > 1024\text{px}$ using integer bilinear sampling.
* Converts RGBA to BT.601 Grayscale ($Y = 0.299R + 0.587G + 0.114B$).
* Reversible: preserves `clampScale` in image metadata for coordinate un-clamping.

### 4.2 Optical Quality Assessment (`ImageQualityAnalyzer.js`)
* **Sharpness**: 3x3 discrete Laplacian kernel variance evaluated over the central 80% ROI. Minimum threshold: 45.0.
* **Illumination**: Mean luminance histogram. Underexposed below 40; overexposed above 215. Optimal window: 100–160.
* **Contrast**: Standard deviation of pixel values. Minimum threshold: 25.0.
* **Specular Glare**: Pixels with $Y \ge 250$. Fails if glare exceeds 4% of total area.
* **Framing Clearance**: Border margin sampling. Lateral/distal margins trigger clipping at $>25\%$ skin pixel density; bottom margin allows natural wrist emergence up to 65%.

### 4.3 Hand Localization & Landmarks (`HandDetector.js`)
* Detects skin chrominance locus in RGB space:
  $$R > 45, \quad G > 30, \quad B > 20, \quad R > G > B, \quad \max(R,G,B) - \min(R,G,B) > 12, \quad |R - G| \ge 8$$
* Computes bounding box and skin mass centroid $(C_x, C_y)$.
* Synthesizes 21 canonical MediaPipe-compatible anatomical landmarks.
* **P5.2 Finding**: Current detector handles single hand bounding boxes; requires multi-hand disambiguation, hand scale validation (too small / too large), and extreme hand tilt validation.

### 4.4 Palm Segmentation & Rectification (`PalmSegmentation.js`)
* Computes orientation angle $\theta = \operatorname{atan2}(dx, dy)$ from Wrist (Landmark 0) to Middle MCP (Landmark 9).
* Samples $512\times 512$ canonical patch rotated by $-\theta$ around palm center.
* Applies elliptical palmar falloff mask $(u^2 / \text{span}^2 + v^2 / \text{height}^2 \le 0.65)$ to exclude outer hand boundary and background artifacts.

### 4.5 Crease Enhancement (`CreaseEnhancementEngine.js`)
* Local CLAHE on an $8\times 8$ grid with clip limit 2.5.
* Multi-scale 2nd Gaussian derivative (Hessian matrix) valley response across fine ($\sigma=1.0$, weight 0.25), medium ($\sigma=2.0$, weight 0.50), and coarse ($\sigma=3.5$, weight 0.25) scales.
* Computes maximum eigenvalue $\lambda_2$ directional valley response, normalized to $[0, 1]$.

### 4.6 Topology & Skeletonization (`CreaseCandidateGraph.js`)
* Adaptive binarization $(\tau = 0.18)$.
* Zhang-Suen morphological thinning to 1-pixel centerlines.
* Graph traversal with 8-connectivity, branch-point detection, spur pruning ($<12\text{px}$), and Douglas-Peucker simplification $(\epsilon = 1.5\text{px})$.

### 4.7 Major Line Classification (`MajorLineClassifier.js`)
* Matches candidates against anatomical prior zones for Life, Head, Heart, Fate, Sun lines.
* Enforces trajectory orientation & span gating:
  * Fate/Sun lines require vertical span $dy \ge 0.18$ and $dy \ge 0.85 dx$.
  * Head/Heart lines require horizontal span $dx \ge 0.18$ and $dx \ge 0.70 dy$.
* Strict honest output: Missing or weak lines return `status: "NOT_DETECTED"` and empty polyline `[]`. Zero decorative hallucinations.

2. Hessian scale weighting ($\sigma \in \{1.0, 2.0, 3.5\}$).

4. Epistemic 4-layer separation (Physical Observation $\to$ Traditional Attribution $\to$ Kai RegAI Synthesis).
5. Zero LLM in CV pipeline (LLM is strictly text synthesis downstream).
6. Non-hallucination invariant: absence of crease evidence returns `NOT_DETECTED`.

## 8. Proposed P5.2 Extension Points

P5.2 will introduce modular hardening around the validated pipeline without breaking existing interfaces:

1. **Resolution & Aspect Ratio Hardening**:
   * Support $480\text{p}, 720\text{p}, 1080\text{p}, 1440\text{p}$ and ratios $4:3, 16:9, 1:1, 3:4, 9:16$ with invariant coordinate transforms.
2. **Retake Decision Engine (`src/cv/quality/RetakeDecisionEngine.js`)**:
   * Additive module evaluating quality, hand localization, palm coverage, and lighting.
   * Produces structured, non-technical actionable guidance (`"Move your palm closer"`, `"Avoid direct glare on the palm"`, `"Keep the entire palm inside the frame"`, etc.).
3. **Palm Usability & Partial-Palm Gating (`src/cv/quality/PalmUsabilityValidator.js`)**:
   * Computes palm area fraction ($\tau_{\text{min}} = 0.10, \tau_{\text{max}} = 0.88$).
   * Validates partial-palm scenarios: distinguishes between a line absent vs cut off / outside camera FOV.
4. **Hand Detection Hardening (`src/cv/detection/HandDetector.js`)**:
   * Detection of multi-hand ambiguity: if multiple discrete skin masses exist, choose primary or request user correction.
   * Rotation and tilt assessment: detect when hand is rotated beyond acceptable rectification angle ($> 40^\circ$).
5. **Confidence Decomposition (`src/cv/crease/MajorLineClassifier.js`)**:
   * Decompose line confidence into interpretable facets: `{ detection, geometry, continuity, imageQuality, overall }`.
6. **Robustness Test Matrix (`tests/cv/p5_2_robustness_matrix.test.js`)**:
   * Comprehensive automated test matrix validating resolution, aspect ratio, lighting extremes, skin chrominance variation, multi-hand detection, partial palm, and retake recommendations.
