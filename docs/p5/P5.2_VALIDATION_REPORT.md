### 3. Changes Implemented in P5.2

#### 3.1 Hardening Modules Introduced
1. **`src/cv/quality/RetakeDecisionEngine.js`**:
   Deterministic policy engine translating optical metrics and positioning flags into actionable user messages (*"Hold your hand steady."*, *"Avoid direct glare on the palm."*, *"Keep the entire palm inside the frame."*, *"Use brighter, even lighting."*, *"Place your palm flat and upright facing the camera."*).
2. **`src/cv/quality/PalmUsabilityValidator.js`**:
   Evaluates palm coverage ($0.10 \le A \le 0.88$), tilt angle from central moments, border clipping, and anatomical region visibility (`visibleRegions`).
3. **`tests/cv/p5_2_robustness_matrix.test.js`**:
   Automated 10-dimensional real-world robustness matrix verifying resolution, aspect ratios, scale, chrominance, lighting extremes, tilt, multi-hand disambiguation, out-of-frame field discrimination, confidence decomposition, and runtime latency.

#### 3.2 Additive Pipeline Hardening
1. **`HandDetector.js`**:
   * Added spatial column projection peak-and-valley cluster analysis for multi-hand disambiguation (`analyzeSpatialClusters`).
   * Added central second-moment blob orientation analysis with a $\pm 14^\circ$ upright deadband, guaranteeing bit-identical compatibility with P5.1 while enabling tilt auto-rectification up to $30^\circ$.
   * Added scale validation emitting `PALM_TOO_FAR` and `PALM_TOO_CLOSE`.
2. **`MajorLineClassifier.js`**:
   * Decomposed line evidence into interpretable facets: `detection`, `geometry`, `continuity`, `imageQuality`, `localization`, `overall`, and `fieldVisibility`.
   * Enforced Apollo corridor constraints ($x \in [0.50, 0.76]$, upper termination $y \le 0.48$) and Saturn corridor constraints ($x \in [0.25, 0.75]$, vertical span $\Delta y \ge 0.25$).
   * Implemented `isTrueFurrow` cross-sectional ridge-vs-edge discrimination to reject monotonic step edges from shadows and hand silhouettes.
3. **`PalmVisionEngine.js`**:
   * Integrated usability validation and retake decision generation into the canonical inference pipeline.
   * Attached developer diagnostics (`diagnostics`) while preserving clean user-facing view models.
   * Bumped semantic engine version to `p5.2.0`.

### 8. Known Limitations & Remaining Risks
1. **Extreme Palm Angle $> 60^\circ$**: Extreme oblique foreshortening alters planar metric distances; correctly gated by `PalmUsabilityValidator` as `EXCESSIVE_TILT`.
2. **Dense Palm Wrinkling (Simian / Secondary Creases)**: Extremely weathered or aged palms contain dense secondary lines; current Douglas-Peucker thresholds prioritize primary crease continuity.
